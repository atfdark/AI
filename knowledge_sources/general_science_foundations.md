# General Science Foundations

This local knowledge pack gives the assistant a broad base for core educational questions.
It is not complete knowledge of all human topics, but it is a practical foundation.

## Mathematics

Mathematics studies patterns, structure, quantity, and change.

Core branches:
- Arithmetic: operations on numbers.
- Algebra: symbols and equations.
- Geometry: shapes, sizes, and spatial relationships.
- Calculus: continuous change, derivatives, and integrals.
- Statistics and probability: uncertainty, data, and inference.

Why mathematics was created:
- To count and trade.
- To measure land and build structures.
- To model nature and support science and engineering.

How mathematics is used:
- Cryptography, finance, machine learning, control systems, optimization, and simulation.

## Physics

Physics studies matter, energy, motion, and fundamental interactions.

Main areas:
- Classical mechanics: forces and motion.
- Thermodynamics: heat, energy transfer, entropy.
- Electromagnetism: electricity, magnetism, waves.
- Quantum mechanics: behavior of very small systems.
- Relativity: space-time, gravity, and high-speed effects.

Why physics was created:
- To explain natural phenomena and predict behavior.
- To design reliable technology from first principles.

How physics works in practice:
- Build a model, derive equations, test predictions with experiments, refine the model.

## Chemistry

Chemistry studies substances, their composition, properties, and reactions.

Core ideas:
- Atomic structure and bonding.
- Molecular interactions.
- Reaction kinetics and equilibrium.
- Acids, bases, redox, and electrochemistry.

Purpose of chemistry:
- Understand transformations of matter.
- Create medicines, materials, fuels, and industrial processes.

## Biology

Biology studies living systems from cells to ecosystems.

Core areas:
- Cell biology and genetics.
- Physiology and development.
- Evolution and ecology.
- Microbiology and immunology.

Why biology matters:
- To explain life processes.
- To improve medicine, agriculture, and public health.

## Engineering and Technology

Engineering applies scientific knowledge to solve practical problems.

Typical workflow:
1. Define requirements and constraints.
2. Model and design.
3. Build prototypes.
4. Test and iterate.
5. Deploy and maintain.

Purpose of technological creation:
- Increase capability, safety, efficiency, and quality of life.
- Solve real-world constraints under cost and reliability limits.

## Scientific Method and Creation Purpose

How things are created in science and engineering:
- Observe problem or phenomenon.
- Form hypothesis or requirements.
- Build model or design.
- Experiment and validate.
- Improve with evidence.

Why creations exist:
- Every artifact usually has a purpose function.
- The purpose is shaped by needs, constraints, and trade-offs.

Typical explanatory dimensions:
- What it is.
- How it works.
- Why it was created.
- What problem it solves.
- What limits and side effects it has.
